Welcome to ARC Car Rental. We believe that renting a car shouldn't mean standing in long lines, deciphering confusing insurance policies, or dealing with surprise fees at the counter. Whether you are traveling for business, planning a family road trip, or need a temporary replacement vehicle, we provide a seamless, stress-free driving experience from start to finish.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 in Calamba City, Laguna, ARC Car Rental started with a simple realization: the traditional car rental process was broken. Corporate chains had lost touch with personal customer service. We began with just 5 vehicles and a mission to bring transparency and local hospitality back to the industry. Today, our fleet has grown, but our core philosophy remains exactly the same—treating every renter like a neighbor.
